# Evidence-Governed Computation in Aperture

## Governing law

> **No claim should exceed the authority of its evidence.**

Aperture separates the evidence plane, authority plane, and presentation plane so a surface cannot become a second source of evidentiary authority.

## Authority layers

Useful authority distinctions include:

- source authority,
- method authority,
- claim authority,
- presentation authority,
- recommendation authority,
- preservation authority.

No lower layer automatically grants a higher one.

## Claim status

Claims may be admissible, conditional, provisional, disputed, superseded, withheld, not computable, or prohibited depending on the governing contract.

## Presentation rule

A dashboard label, chart, tooltip, summary, explanation, export, or downstream agent output has no independent evidentiary authority. Authoritative content must remain bound to admitted evidence and claim scope.
