# Evidence Commons

Evidence Commons is the preservation initiative for Runtime Evidence that must survive beyond the originating Aperture interface.

## Preservation principle

**Aperture produces the evidence. Evidence Commons gives that evidence a life beyond Aperture.**

A preserved evidence artifact may carry:

- run/canonical identity,
- provenance and source relationships,
- recorder frames/events,
- telemetry and temporal markers,
- bounded findings,
- Passport imprint,
- claim boundary and missingness,
- method/version references,
- evidence-flow and authority metadata.

A Passport preserves the identity and limits of examination. It does not certify the authenticity or objective truth of the original source merely because the artifact is sealed.
